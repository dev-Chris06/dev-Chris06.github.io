Contact form privacy

Here is what happens to the information submitted through this portfolio’s contact form.

Information requested

The form asks for your name, email address, a subject, and your message. This information is used to understand your request and reply to you.

Sent through FormSubmit

The form sends this information to FormSubmit, which forwards it to Christopher AHO GLELE’s inbox. FormSubmit says it retains submissions for 30 days. The delivered email may then remain in the inbox according to that mailbox’s settings.
